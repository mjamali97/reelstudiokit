# Idea → script → style → edit · the runbook

**Trigger:** the owner sends an idea (a product, a service, a question customers ask, an offer, a topic, one line). Also `/reel-idea`.
**The flow:** verify → script with hooks (Hormozi's model) + a viral score → two or three edit styles with their odds → they
film and pick → edit (`STYLE-REEL.md`) → QA (`MOTION-QA.md`) → they approve → `/publish`.
Never start editing before they have the script and picked a style. Read `profile/CREATOR.md`, `VOICE.md`, `HOOKS.md`,
`RULES.md` and `IDEAS-REMOVED.md` first.

## Step 1 · Verify the idea (before writing a word)

- Read their business playbook (`playbook/business/<file>`) and check the facts with `BUSINESS-REEL.md` §1: the real price,
  the real hours, what is actually in stock, claims they can stand behind. Only what is true today.
- **The distance ladder** — the first filter on every idea, before format and before hook. Does the viewer touch this?
  | tier | the subject is… | verdict |
  |---|---|---|
  | 0 | the creator's own hands, when the thing it does is tier 1 for the viewer | strongest |
  | 1 | something the viewer uses or wants every day (food, money, their city, their looks, their family, their phone) | go |
  | 2 | something they use or want sometimes | go with a strong hook |
  | 3 | a good story with no relationship to them | at most one a week |
  | 4 | far from them (an industry insider topic, a foreign brand's story) | do not make it |
  Score what it does for the viewer, not the thing: "our new oven" is tier 4, "why your birthday cake arrives still
  cold and firm" is tier 1. The ladder belongs to the audience: score again for each language/market they post in.
  If the idea is far, say so and offer a closer angle before scripting.
- **Can this viewer act on it tonight, from where they are, with what they have?** For some audiences the trigger is
  access (delivery area, opening hours, payment options, language), not price. Write the audience's real blocker in
  `profile/CREATOR.md` and test every idea against it.
- Already in `profile/IDEAS-REMOVED.md`? Drop it silently and offer something else.

## Step 2 · The script

Monologue in their voice (`profile/VOICE.md`): read two of their latest takes or SRTs first and match how their sentences
actually run. One speaker, no second voice. The first sentence is the result or the call-out. The close is their own gate
line (e.g. «اگه X رو می‌خوای، فقط کافیه کامنت کنی «Y» تا واست بفرستمش»).

### Alex Hormozi's model, applied (*$100M Leads*, *$100M Offers*)
- **Hook → Retain → Reward.** The hook earns the next 3 seconds; retain with an open loop and a counted list; the reward is
  the thing itself (the dish, the result, the answer), then the free gate.
- **Hook = a call-out or a big claim:** "if you are X / have Y…" (who it is for) or a true number that sounds wrong.
  Never a greeting or their name.
- **Value equation:** dream outcome × perceived likelihood (proof: the real product, real customers, real numbers)
  ÷ (time delay × effort). Say the time and the effort out loud when they are small and true.
- **Proof beats promises:** one real number with its source on screen. Never «خیلی‌ها», «همه».
- **Give the best thing away** in the gate (the menu, the price list, the recipe, the guide, the checklist); the comment word is
  the lead magnet.

### Hook patterns (general; replace with `profile/HOOKS.md` once the account has data)
| pattern | example shape |
|---|---|
| call-out + stakes | «اگه … داری / هستی، قبل از … حتماً اینو ببین» |
| stacked numbers | «این … [عدد] تا … داره، [عدد] نفر … و …» (three true numbers in one breath) |
| result on frame one | the finished thing (the cake cut open, the tool's output) is on screen at 0 s |
| call-out + daily pain | «اگه هر روز داری … می‌کنی، …» |
| contrarian | «… از خود … مهم‌تره» |
| promise + no cost | weak alone; pair it with access or proof |

### Script shape (five beats)
| beat | time | job |
|---|---|---|
| [hook] | 0–2 s | call-out or number; the result already on screen |
| [gap] | 2–6 s | the pain they have («نمی‌دونی…؟») |
| [turn] | 6–25 s | the product/service/answer, named, with a counted list |
| [proof] | 25–45 s | one real number, review (consent) or result, shown |
| [return] | last 3 s | the gate word, spoken |

Deliver two or three hook variants, the full script under the best one, and the gate word. Beat tags are for the editor,
never spoken.

## Step 3 · The viral score (out of 10, said with the script)

| part | points | full marks when |
|---|---|---|
| Distance | 0–3 | tier 0–1; the viewer touches it this week |
| Hook | 0–2 | call-out or number, result on screen by second 2 |
| Proof | 0–2 | a real number/page/customer shown, not said |
| Reward | 0–1 | a free, tangible gate (menu, price list, guide, booking link, code) |
| Novelty | 0–1 | not already covered by big accounts in their language this month |
| Length | 0–1 | 35–60 s with no dead second |

Say which part is weakest and how to raise it. These weights are a starting point: after every ten posts, `/analytics`
compares scores with real saves/shares/follows per 1k views and re-weights this table (write the change and the date here).

## Step 4 · Two or three styles, with odds

`/reel-style`: one pick and one or two alternatives for this content, each with its score and one line of why. Combine the
style score with the content score honestly; a weak idea is not saved by a style.

## Step 5 · Edit

After they film and pick: `TAKE.md` (cut + SRT + audio) → `STYLE-REEL.md` (the look) → `MOTION-QA.md` → their approval →
`CAPTION.md`, `COVER-AND-GATE.md`, `PUBLISH.md`. Plan the week with `/content-plan`.
