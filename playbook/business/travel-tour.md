# Travel agency / tour operator / tour guide · content playbook

## The customer
- Dreams of a trip but worries about cost, documents, safety and getting it wrong.
- Chooses on: the total real price, what is included, trust (licence, past trips), the guide, dates.
- Stops on: hidden costs, visa/document confusion, fear of a bad hotel, too many options.

## Content pillars (weekly mix)
| pillar | share | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| the place | 30 % | one destination in 40 s: what to see, eat, when to go |
| real cost | 20 % | «سفر [مقصد] با [مبلغ]: چی شامل می‌شه، چی نه» |
| how-to & documents | 20 % | steps, timelines, what to pack, common mistakes (no legal promises) |
| on our tours | 20 % | travellers' moments (consent), the guide, the hotel as it really is |
| offers | 10 % | departures with real dates and seats |

## Reel ideas
1. «سفر [مقصد]، ۵ روز، کل هزینه» — cost breakdown.
2. «۳ تا جای [مقصد] که توی تورهای معمولی نیست» — insider.
3. «قبل از سفر خارجی این ۵ تا رو چک کن» — checklist.
4. «هتل تور ما دقیقاً این شکلیه» — honesty.
5. «بهترین وقت سفر به [مقصد]» — timing.
6. «اشتباهی که سفر رو گرون‌تر می‌کنه» — mistake.
7. «یه روز از تور [مقصد]» — on tour.
8. «چی ببندیم واسه [فصل/مقصد]» — packing.
9. «سؤال مسافرها: [سؤال]» — answered.
10. «تور [تاریخ]، [تعداد] صندلی مونده» — offer, true.

## Weekly shot list
Arrivals, landmarks at golden hour, food close-ups, the hotel room as it is, the bus/plane, the guide explaining,
travellers laughing (consent), the map with the route.

## Proof
Agency licence (verifiable), past tours with dates, real hotel photos, written inclusions/exclusions, reviews (consent).

## Gate words
| word | they get |
|---|---|
| هزینه | the full cost breakdown of the reel's trip |
| چک‌لیست | the pre-travel checklist |
| تور | upcoming departures and how to book |

## Stories
Live from tours, polls for the next destination, seats left, Q&A.

## Never
Visa or entry guarantees, prices that hide taxes/fees, stock photos as "our hotel", travellers without consent.

## Styles that fit
`/vogue` for destinations, `/vox` for cost breakdowns, `/doodle` for documents and packing.
