# Language school / tutoring / exam prep (IELTS, university entrance, school subjects) · content playbook

## The customer
- A learner (or a parent) with a deadline: an exam, a migration plan, a job, a school grade.
- Chooses on: results of past students, the teacher's clarity, method, price, class size, online or in person.
- Stops on: "I've tried and failed before", price unknown, fear of the level test, no time.

## Content pillars (weekly mix)
| pillar | share | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| a mini-lesson | 35 % | one word, rule, trick or exam tip fully taught in 40 s |
| common mistakes | 20 % | «۳ تا اشتباه رایج توی [مهارت]» |
| results | 15 % | students' scores/acceptances with consent, the timeline |
| the method & class | 15 % | how a class runs, the level test, materials |
| practical | 15 % | courses, schedules, prices, next start date |

## Reel ideas
1. «این جمله رو ۹۰ درصد اشتباه می‌گن» — mistake.
2. «یه ترفند واسه [بخش آزمون]» — exam tip.
3. «از سطح [x] به [y] توی [مدت]: یکی از شاگردهامون» — result (consent).
4. «۵ تا کلمه که توی [موقعیت] لازمت می‌شه» — useful list.
5. «تعیین سطح دقیقاً چطوریه؟» — removes fear.
6. «برنامه‌ی روزانه‌ی ۳۰ دقیقه‌ای» — routine.
7. «چرا گرامر خوندن تنها جواب نمی‌ده» — contrarian, with reasons.
8. «سؤال این هفته‌ی شاگردها» — answered.
9. «یه جلسه‌ی کلاس، توی ۴۰ ثانیه» — the class.
10. «ثبت‌نام دوره‌ی [ماه]» — offer with date.

## Weekly shot list
Teacher at the board, writing the rule, students working (consent), the book/material, the screen of an online class,
a score report (name hidden unless consent), the building/classroom.

## Proof
Real score reports with consent, number of students, teacher's certificates (verifiable), sample lesson.

## Gate words
| word | they get |
|---|---|
| لغت | a word list PDF for the reel's topic |
| برنامه | a study plan |
| تعیین‌سطح | the free level-test link |

## Stories
Word of the day, a quiz sticker, Q&A, class schedule and free seats.

## Never
Guaranteed scores, students' results without consent, misleading claims about visas or admissions, fake "native" claims.

## Styles that fit
`/doodle` for lessons, `/swiss` for mistakes and lists, `/vox` for a student's result story.
