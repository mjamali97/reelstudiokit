# Weddings & events (venue, planner, catering, decoration, DJ, ceremony services) · content playbook

## The customer
- A couple or a family planning one big day; overwhelmed by choices, budgets and family opinions.
- Chooses on: seeing real events they did, the vibe, the total price, reliability on the day, reviews.
- Stops on: hidden costs, fear something will go wrong, unclear packages, not seeing their style.

## Content pillars (weekly mix)
| pillar | share | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| real events | 30 % | an event from setup to the first dance (couple's consent) |
| planning help | 25 % | timelines, budgets, checklists, what to ask a venue |
| setup / behind the scenes | 20 % | the decoration going up, the kitchen, the team at 6 a.m. |
| packages & prices | 15 % | what each package really includes |
| reviews & people | 10 % | the team, couples' messages (consent) |

## Reel ideas
1. «این سالن از خالی تا شب مراسم» — setup time-lapse.
2. «۵ تا سؤالی که قبل از قرارداد تالار باید بپرسی» — checklist.
3. «با [بودجه] چه مراسمی می‌شه گرفت» — budget.
4. «جدول زمانی شب عروسی» — planning.
5. «اشتباهی که هزینه‌ی مراسم رو بالا می‌بره» — mistake.
6. «پکیج [x] دقیقاً چی داره» — transparency.
7. «پشت‌صحنه‌ی آشپزخونه‌ی یه مراسم ۳۰۰ نفره» — behind the scenes.
8. «یه مراسم کوچیک و صمیمی» — another style.
9. «پیام عروس و داماد بعد از مراسم (با اجازه)» — proof.
10. «تاریخ‌های خالی فصل [x]» — offer.

## Weekly shot list
Empty venue → decorated (same angle), table setting close-ups, lights switching on, the kitchen plating, the team
briefing, the entrance, the dance floor (consent), flowers being placed.

## Proof
Real events (consent), written package contents, reviews, licences/permits where required, years and number of events.

## Gate words
| word | they get |
|---|---|
| پکیج | packages and prices |
| چک‌لیست | the wedding planning checklist |
| تاریخ | free dates + booking |

## Stories
Setups in progress, polls between two decorations, free dates, Q&A.

## Never
Couples or guests without consent, another company's events as theirs, prices without what is excluded.

## Styles that fit
`/vogue` (the natural fit), `/doodle` for planning guides, `/swiss` for prices and checklists.
