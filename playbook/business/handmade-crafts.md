# Handmade / crafts / art (ceramics, candles, leather, knitting, painting, صنایع دستی) · content playbook

## The customer
- Buys a unique object for themselves or a gift; pays for the hands and the story behind it.
- Chooses on: the making process, uniqueness, quality, price that feels fair for the work, packaging.
- Stops on: "why is it this price?", fear it is mass-produced, delivery risk for fragile pieces.

## Content pillars (weekly mix)
| pillar | share | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| the making | 40 % | satisfying process from raw material to finished piece |
| the finished piece | 20 % | in use, in a home, in light |
| why it costs this | 15 % | hours, materials, steps: honest |
| learn a little | 15 % | a simple technique, care for the piece, a workshop |
| practical | 10 % | how to order, custom orders, shipping fragile items |

## Reel ideas

3. «سفارشی‌ترین کار این ماه» — the story.
4. «یه تکنیک ساده که خودت می‌تونی امتحان کنی» — learn.
5. «بسته‌بندی سفارش‌های شکستنی» — trust.
6. «از نزدیک ببین» — macro.
7. «اشتباهی که کار رو خراب کرد (و چی یاد گرفتم)» — story.

9. «یه کار، سه رنگ» — options.
10. «اینجا کار می‌کنم» — the studio.

## Weekly shot list
Hands on the material, tools in action, the slow reveal (kiln door, mould opening), drying/curing, the finished piece in
daylight, packing, the workspace.

## Proof
The full process on camera, hours and materials stated, customers' photos of pieces in their homes (consent).

## Gate words
| word | they get |
|---|---|
| قیمت | catalogue + prices + how to order |
| سفارشی | how custom orders work |
| کارگاه | workshop dates and booking |

## Stories
Work in progress, polls between colours, restocks, packaging today's orders.

## Never
Reselling mass-produced items as handmade, copying another artist's designs, hiding shipping costs.

## Styles that fit
`/vogue` for signature pieces, `/doodle` for techniques, `/vox` for the "why it costs this" story.
