# Hotel / eco-lodge (بوم‌گردی) / guesthouse / villa rental · content playbook

## The customer
- Picks a place for a trip, a weekend escape or an occasion; compares photos, price and reviews.
- Chooses on: does it look like the photos, the experience (food, nature, hosts), cleanliness, price per night, location.
- Stops on: fear the photos lie, hidden fees, hard to reach, unclear booking.

## Content pillars (weekly mix)
| pillar | share | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| the experience | 30 % | waking up there, breakfast, the view, the evening: one feeling per reel |
| the room as it is | 20 % | an honest room tour, the bathroom, what's included |
| the area | 20 % | what to do nearby, how to get there, best season |
| the hosts & guests | 15 % | the people, local food, guests' moments (consent) |
| practical | 15 % | prices per season, booking, policies |

## Reel ideas
1. «یه صبح اینجا این‌جوری شروع می‌شه» — the experience.
2. «اتاق [نوع]، بدون فیلتر» — honest tour.
3. «صبحونه‌ی محلی‌مون چی داره» — food.
4. «از [شهر] تا اینجا چطوری بیای» — directions.
5. «۳ تا کار نزدیک ما» — the area.
6. «قیمت هر شب توی [فصل]» — prices.
7. «بهترین فصل اومدن» — timing.
8. «مهمون‌هامون چی گفتن (با اجازه)» — proof.
9. «یه روز از کار میزبان» — behind the scenes.
10. «رزرو کردن دقیقاً چطوریه» — practical.

## Weekly shot list
Sunrise from the window, the bed made, breakfast being served, steam from tea, the courtyard, the path/road arriving,
the host welcoming, night lights, nature sounds (record clean audio).

## Proof
Unfiltered room footage, real reviews (consent) or booking-site ratings (current), licence where required, prices in
writing.

## Gate words
| word | they get |
|---|---|
| قیمت | prices by season + booking link |
| مسیر | the route guide |
| برنامه | a 2-day itinerary for the area |

## Stories
Free dates this week, the view today, a poll (which experience next), Q&A.

## Never
Edited photos that change the room, prices without taxes/fees, guests without consent, unbookable "offers".

## Styles that fit
`/vogue` for the experience, `/doodle` for routes and itineraries, `/swiss` for prices and dates.
