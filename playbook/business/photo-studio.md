# Photo studio (آتلیه) / photographer · content playbook

## The customer
- Books for a moment: wedding, engagement, newborn, kids, family, graduation, a portrait, product photos for a shop.
- Chooses on: the style of the results, how they will look (posing, light), the package price, the delivery time.
- Stops on: "I'm not photogenic", price unknown, fear of the session, not seeing results like their own situation.

## Content pillars (weekly mix)
| pillar | share | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| before → after | 30 % | the raw frame → the edit, or the set → the final photo |
| behind the shot | 25 % | the light setup, posing a nervous client, the trick that made the shot |
| the client's moment | 20 % | (consent!) reactions to the photos, a family session |
| packages & booking | 15 % | what each package includes, how long, delivery time |
| tips | 10 % | what to wear, how to prepare, best time of day for outdoor shoots |

## Reel ideas
1. «این عکس رو با یه چراغ و یه دیوار ساده گرفتیم» — setup → result.
2. «اگه فکر می‌کنی جلوی دوربین معذبی، این سه تا ژست رو امتحان کن» — posing tips.
3. «فایل خام، فایل نهایی» — before/after slider.
4. «چی بپوشیم واسه عکس خانوادگی؟» — colours, patterns, shoes.
5. «یه جلسه‌ی عکاسی نوزاد از اول تا آخر» — (consent) calm, safe handling.
6. «پکیج‌ها دقیقاً چی دارن؟» — the list, prices or «قیمت تو دایرکت».
7. «یه لوکیشن مخفی توی [شهر] واسه عکس بیرونی» — the place.
8. «سه تا اشتباه قبل از عکس عروسی» — preparation.
9. «عکس محصول با گوشی در مقابل استودیو» — for shop owners.
10. «واکنش مشتری وقتی آلبومش رو دید» — consent.

## Weekly shot list
The flash firing, the softbox being moved, the photographer's hands on the camera, the client laughing (consent), the
screen with the edit, the printed album being opened, the studio sets, the backdrop changing.

## Proof
Before/after, real albums, the number of sessions or years, a client's message (consent), the equipment honestly.

## Gate words
| word | they get |
|---|---|
| پکیج | packages and prices |
| ژست | a posing guide PDF |
| لباس | what-to-wear guide |

## Stories
A frame from today's session (consent), poll between two edits, open booking slots this week, a question box.

## Never
Post clients' photos (especially children) without written consent, edit body shapes beyond what the client asked,
show work that is not theirs.

## Styles that fit
`/vogue` (editorial, the natural fit), `/vox` for a session's story, `/doodle` for tips.
