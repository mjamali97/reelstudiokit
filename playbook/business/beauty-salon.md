# Beauty salon / hair / nails / makeup / barber · content playbook

## The customer
- Books when they see a result on someone like them; loyal once they trust the hands.
- Chooses on: results (before/after), hygiene, price, time it takes, how long it lasts, the vibe.
- Stops on: fear of a bad result, price unknown, hygiene doubts, "is it for my hair/skin type?".

## Content pillars (weekly mix)
| pillar | share | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| transformation | 35 % | before → after, the reveal, the mirror moment (consent) |
| process | 25 % | the technique step by step, the tools, the timing |
| care & honesty | 20 % | how long it lasts, aftercare, who it is NOT for |
| prices & booking | 10 % | services list, duration, booking slots |
| hygiene & team | 10 % | sterilising tools, the team, the space |

## Reel ideas
1. «از این، به این، توی [مدت]» — transformation.
2. «این مدل واسه کدوم صورت/مو خوبه؟» — who it suits.
3. «سه تا کاری که بعد از [خدمت] نباید بکنی» — aftercare, counted.
4. «ابزارمون رو این‌جوری استریل می‌کنیم» — hygiene proof.
5. «قیمت [خدمت] چرا این‌قدر فرق می‌کنه؟» — honest price explanation.
6. «ترند [مدل] رو امتحان کردیم» — trend, real client (consent).
7. «یه روز توی سالن» — time-lapse.
8. «چقدر دووم میاره؟ روز اول و روز [n]م» — honesty.
9. «سؤال پرتکرار: [موضوع]» — the answer.
10. «نوبت‌های خالی این هفته» — booking.

## Weekly shot list
Mirror reveal, the brush/clipper/nail file in action, product application close-up, tools in the steriliser, the chair
spinning, the price board, the client's smile (consent), hands washing.

## Proof
Before/after in the same light and angle, the duration counted on screen, hygiene steps, certificates for regulated
services, return clients (consent).

## Gate words
| word | they get |
|---|---|
| نوبت | booking link/number + free slots |
| قیمت | the price list |
| مراقبت | the aftercare guide |

## Never
Filters on before/after, results from another salon, medical or skin-treatment claims beyond their licence, clients
without consent.

## Styles that fit
`/vogue` for transformations, `/swiss` for prices and "3 mistakes", `/doodle` for aftercare.
