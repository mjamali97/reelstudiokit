# Jewellery / gold / watches / accessories · content playbook

## The customer
- Buys for a gift, an engagement or wedding, an investment, or themselves; trust is everything.
- Chooses on: design, trust in purity/authenticity, the price breakdown (material + making charge), after-sales.
- Stops on: fear of overpaying or fakes, price changes daily, not knowing what is fair.

## Content pillars (weekly mix)
| pillar | share | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| the piece up close | 30 % | macro shots, on a hand/neck, how it catches light |
| buyer education | 30 % | how the price is made, purity marks, how to spot a fake, care |
| occasions | 15 % | engagement, gifts, sets |
| the craft | 15 % | design, making, setting a stone |
| practical | 10 % | how to order, today's price note, guarantees |

## Reel ideas
1. «قیمت یه انگشتر دقیقاً از چی ساخته می‌شه؟» — price breakdown (with today's date).
2. «عیار رو این‌جوری چک کن» — authenticity.
3. «سه تا اشتباه موقع خرید حلقه» — mistakes.
4. «این گردنبند رو از نزدیک ببین» — the piece.
5. «با [مبلغ] چه هدیه‌ای می‌شه گرفت» — gift guide.
6. «ساخت یه انگشتر از طرح تا ویترین» — craft.
7. «طلای آب‌شده یا نو؟» — education.
8. «جواهرت رو این‌جوری تمیز کن» — care.
9. «ست عقد این هفته (با اجازه)» — the story.
10. «ضمانت و تعویض پیش ما چطوریه» — trust.

## Weekly shot list
Macro of the piece rotating on a turntable, on a hand in daylight, the scale with weight, the hallmark close-up, the
craftsman's hands, the gift box opening, the shop counter.

## Proof
Hallmarks/certificates shown, weight on the scale, a written price breakdown, guarantee terms, years in business.

## Gate words
| word | they get |
|---|---|
| قیمت | how today's price is calculated + the piece's price |
| عیار | the authenticity checklist |
| هدیه | the gift guide by budget |

## Stories
New pieces, a poll between two designs, Q&A, opening hours.

## Never
Investment promises («حتماً گرون می‌شه»), prices without the date (gold prices change daily), unverifiable purity claims,
clients' faces without consent.

## Styles that fit
`/vogue` (the natural fit), `/vox` for price breakdowns with numbers, `/swiss` for mistakes.
