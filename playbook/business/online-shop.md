# Online shop (clothes, accessories, home goods, cosmetics, handmade…) · content playbook

## The customer
- Scrolls, saves, compares, asks the price in the DM, buys when trust and price are clear.
- Chooses on: how it looks on a real person / in a real room, quality up close, price, size fit, delivery and returns.
- Stops on: "will it look like this on me?", size doubts, shipping cost/time, fear of a scam, price hidden.

## Content pillars (weekly mix)
| pillar | share | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| on a real person / in a real space | 30 % | try-on, styling, the product in use |
| close-up quality | 20 % | fabric, stitching, material, packaging |
| trust | 20 % | packing real orders, shipping proof, honest reviews (consent), the return policy |
| how to choose | 20 % | size guide, which colour for whom, three ways to wear it |
| new arrivals & offers | 10 % | drops, restocks, real-deadline offers |

## Reel ideas
1. «یه [محصول]، سه جور ست» — styling.
2. «سایزت رو این‌جوری انتخاب کن» — the size guide on a body/with a tape.
3. «از نزدیک ببین جنسش چیه» — macro shots.
4. «امروز این‌ها رو بسته‌بندی کردیم» — packing orders (no customer data visible).
5. «قیمتش [مبلغ]ه و این‌ها رو داره» — price on screen, transparency.
6. «سه تا سؤال که قبل از خرید آنلاین باید بپرسی» — trust building.
7. «رنگ واقعیش زیر نور روز» — honest colour.
8. «موجودی دوباره اومد» — restock.
9. «مشتری‌ها چی گفتن؟» — real messages (consent, names hidden).
10. «چطوری سفارش بدی (از دایرکت تا دستت)» — the buying path.

## Weekly shot list
Try-on in daylight, the product turning on a surface, fabric close-up, unboxing, packing with tissue/label, the shelf of
stock, the courier pickup, the product in a real room/outfit, price tag shot.

## Proof
Real try-ons (several body types if clothing), real order packing, delivery proof, return policy in writing, reviews
with consent, the business's registration or a known marketplace profile.

## Gate words
| word | they get |
|---|---|
| قیمت | price + link/DM to order |
| سایز | the size guide |
| کد | a first-order discount code with an end date |

## Stories
New arrivals with link sticker, polls between two colours, «سؤال داری؟» box, packing today's orders, restock countdown.

## Captions
Product + one feel line → price (or «قیمت تو دایرکت» only if they insist; shown prices sell more trust) → sizes/colours
→ how to order → delivery time and returns → 5 hashtags.

## Never
Supplier photos presented as own, fake scarcity, hidden fees, customer addresses or names visible in packing videos,
brand names they are not authorised to sell.

## Styles that fit
`/swiss` for prices and drops, `/hud` for "order tracking" style trust reels, `/doodle` for size guides.
