# Bakery / pastry / cake shop · content playbook

## The customer
- Buys for an occasion (birthday, guests, Nowruz, a gift) or a daily treat; orders cakes days ahead.
- Chooses on: how it looks cut open, flavour, freshness, price per kilo, custom designs, delivery on time.
- Stops on: unsure of the price, "will it look like the photo?", how far ahead to order, will it survive the trip.

## Content pillars (weekly mix)
| pillar | share | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| the cut | 30 % | the cake or pastry cut open, the layers, the cream |
| the making | 25 % | piping, glazing, decorating, the oven door, the morning batch |
| ordering made easy | 20 % | sizes, price per kilo, how many people, how early to order, delivery |
| occasions | 15 % | the custom birthday cake of the week, the season's special |
| care & tips | 10 % | how long it keeps, fridge or not, how to cut a cake cleanly |

## Reel ideas
1. «این کیک رو برای [مناسبت] سفارش دادن، ببین از نزدیک چی شد» — design → cut.
2. «یه کیک چند کیلویی واسه چند نفر کافیه؟» — size guide with real cakes, saved a lot.
3. «شیرینی خامه‌ای رو چند روز می‌شه نگه داشت؟» — care facts (true for their recipe).
4. «سه تا اشتباه موقع سفارش کیک تولد» — order too late, wrong size, no photo of the design.
5. «ساعت ۵ صبح اینجا این‌جوریه» — the morning batch.
6. «پرسفروش‌ترین‌های این هفته» — top 3, counted.
7. «با [مبلغ] چی می‌تونی بخری» — a box assembled on camera.
8. «کیکی که عکسش رو آوردن، کیکی که تحویل دادیم» — reference vs result (with consent).
9. «طعم جدید: [اسم]» — the launch.
10. «چطوری کیک رو تمیز ببری» — the tip.

## Weekly shot list
Cutting a cake (top-down and side), piping, glaze pour, cream spread, sprinkles falling, the tray coming out of the
oven, boxes being tied, the shop window, the display fridge, hands placing the topper, the delivery box opened.

## Proof
Real photo request vs delivered cake, the weight on the scale, the price list, repeat customers (consent), on-time
delivery count from their own records.

## Gate words
| word | they get |
|---|---|
| منو | the price list per kilo and per box |
| سفارش | how to order a custom cake + how many days ahead |
| تولد | the size guide (how many kilos for how many people) |

## Stories (daily)
Today's batch with prices, a poll for next week's flavour, "custom cakes still open for Friday?", a question box,
delivery photos (with consent).

## Captions
Product name + one sensory line → price/size → «کامنت کن «منو»» → address, hours, order deadline → 5 hashtags: city +
product + occasion.

## Never
Show a cake they did not make, promise delivery times they cannot keep, health claims («بدون شکر» only if true and
labelled), customers' faces without consent.

## Styles that fit
`/doodle` for size guides and tips, `/swiss` for prices and lists, `/vogue` for a signature cake.
