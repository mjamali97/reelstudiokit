# Gym / fitness coach / yoga / pilates · content playbook

## The customer
- Wants to look or feel different, or not hurt; usually starts after a trigger (a wedding, a photo, a doctor's advice, the new year).
- Chooses on: does this coach get results for people like me, is the place clean and not intimidating, price, schedule, distance.
- Stops on: "I'm too out of shape to start", embarrassment, price unknown, fear of injury, failed attempts before.

## Content pillars (weekly mix)
| pillar | share | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| one exercise done right | 30 % | the move, the common mistake, the fix, for a body part people care about |
| real progress | 20 % | members' journeys (written consent), numbers over time, not only before/after photos |
| myths | 15 % | «۳۰ دقیقه کاردیو چربی شکم رو آب می‌کنه؟» with the honest answer |
| the place & people | 20 % | the coach, a class, the beginner's first day, cleanliness |
| practical | 15 % | prices, schedule, first free session, what to bring |

## Reel ideas
1. «اگه زانوت موقع اسکات درد می‌گیره، احتمالاً این کار رو می‌کنی» — mistake → fix.
2. «۳ تا حرکت واسه کسی که پشت میز کار می‌کنه» — counted, useful at home.
3. «جلسه‌ی اولت پیش ما دقیقاً این شکلیه» — removes fear.
4. «۶ ماه با [عضو] (با اجازه‌ش)» — the journey, numbers.
5. «این باور غلطه: [باور]» — myth.
6. «با ۲۰ دقیقه در روز چی می‌شه» — realistic expectations.
7. «تمرین امروز کلاس [ساعت]» — energy, the class.
8. «سه تا اشتباه تازه‌واردها» — beginners.
9. «قیمت‌ها و ساعت‌ها، بی‌تعارف» — practical.
10. «مربی‌مون کیه؟» — credentials, path.

## Weekly shot list
A move in slow motion (side angle), the mistake vs the right form, the class from above, weights being racked, the coach
cueing, cleaning the equipment, the entrance, a member's high five (consent), the timer, sweat and water bottle.

## Proof
Coach certifications (link to the issuing body), members' progress with written consent and the real timeframe, class
numbers, years open.

## Gate words
| word | they get |
|---|---|
| برنامه | a beginner's 4-week plan (PDF) |
| قیمت | prices + schedule + first-session offer |
| حرکت | a form checklist for the reel's exercise |

## Stories
Today's classes and free spots, a poll (which body part next), member of the week (consent), a 15-second home move.

## Never
Before/after photos without written consent, promises of kilos in days, supplement or diet claims beyond their
qualification, body shaming, editing bodies.

## Styles that fit
`/swiss` for mistakes and myths, `/doodle` for form explanations, `/vogue` for a member's story or the coach.
