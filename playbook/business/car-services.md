# Car services (repair shop, car wash, detailing, tyres, car accessories) · content playbook

## The customer
- Has a problem now (a noise, a warning light, a dirty car before an event) or wants the car to look/feel new.
- Chooses on: honesty (will they overcharge me?), skill, speed, price clarity, before → after.
- Stops on: fear of being ripped off, not knowing the price, distance, waiting time.

## Content pillars (weekly mix)
| pillar | share | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| before → after | 30 % | the wash, the polish, the repair: satisfying transformations |
| diagnose it yourself | 25 % | «این صدا یعنی چی؟», warning lights, simple checks the owner can do |
| honest pricing | 15 % | what a job costs and why, parts original vs aftermarket |
| process | 20 % | the technician at work, tools, the steps |
| the shop | 10 % | team, hours, booking, warranty |

## Reel ideas
1. «اگه ماشینت موقع ترمز این صدا رو می‌ده، این رو ببین» — diagnose.
2. «از این، به این، توی [ساعت]» — detailing transformation.
3. «۳ تا چیزی که قبل از سفر جاده‌ای چک کنی» — checklist.
4. «لنت اصلی یا تجاری؟ فرقش این‌جاست» — honest comparison.
5. «این چراغ روی داشبورد یعنی چی؟» — warning lights.
6. «تعویض روغن دقیقاً چه کارهایی داره» — process.
7. «قیمت [سرویس] پیش ما و چرا» — pricing.
8. «اشتباهی که موتور رو خراب می‌کنه» — mistake.
9. «یه روز توی کارگاه» — time-lapse.
10. «مشتری این‌جوری آورد، این‌جوری برد» — with consent, plate hidden.

## Weekly shot list
Foam on the paint, water rinse in slow motion, polisher on a scratch (before/after split), the engine bay, the lift raising
a car, worn vs new part side by side, the diagnostic screen, the finished car leaving, the team.

## Proof
Before/after in the same light, worn parts shown to the customer, written warranty, prices on a board, reviews (consent).

## Gate words
| word | they get |
|---|---|
| سفر | the pre-trip checklist |
| قیمت | the price list / booking |
| چراغ | the warning-lights guide |

## Stories
Today's cars (plates hidden), free slots, a poll (which service next), a quick tip.

## Never
Number plates or owners' faces without consent, "fixed forever" promises, bad-mouthing parts brands without evidence,
advice that is unsafe to do at home.

## Styles that fit
`/hud` for diagnostics and warning lights, `/swiss` for prices and checklists, `/vogue` for a showroom-level detail job.
