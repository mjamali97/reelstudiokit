# Clinic / doctor / dentist / physio / nutritionist · content playbook

## The customer
- Searches when worried or when something hurts; trusts credentials, clear explanations and calm.
- Chooses on: the person's expertise, explanations they understand, price clarity, location, waiting time.
- Stops on: fear, cost, not knowing if they need a visit, misinformation they already believe.

## Content pillars (weekly mix)
| pillar | share | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| myth vs fact | 30 % | one common belief, the evidence, what to do instead |
| "when to see someone" | 25 % | signs that need a visit vs signs that don't |
| what happens in a visit | 20 % | the room, the device, the steps, how long, does it hurt |
| the person | 15 % | the doctor/therapist, why they do this, credentials shown |
| practical | 10 % | prices or ranges if allowed, booking, insurance |

## Reel ideas
1. «این باور غلطه: [باور]» — myth vs fact with a source.
2. «اگه این سه تا علامت رو داری، وقتشه بیای» — when to see someone.
3. «توی یه جلسه‌ی [درمان] دقیقاً چی می‌شه؟» — walkthrough.
4. «درد داره؟ واقعیتش اینه» — honesty.
5. «این دستگاه چی کار می‌کنه؟» — explain the device.
6. «سؤال این هفته: [سؤال]» — answered.
7. «سه تا کاری که توی خونه می‌تونی بکنی (و یه کاری که نباید)» — safe self-care.
8. «چرا [درمان] قیمتش این‌قدره؟» — cost explained.
9. «مدرکم رو اینجا می‌تونی چک کنی» — verifiable credential (the licence page).
10. «یه روز از کار ما» — the team.

## Weekly shot list
The doctor explaining with a model/diagram, the device working (no patient identifiable), hands washing, the waiting room,
the sterile tray, a whiteboard sketch, the clinic entrance.

## Proof
Licence/registration (the official lookup page), sources for every health claim (guidelines, papers), years of practice,
real cases only anonymised and with written consent.

## Gate words
| word | they get |
|---|---|
| نوبت | booking link/number |
| راهنما | a one-page guide (prep before a visit, aftercare) |

## Never
Promise results or cures, show identifiable patients without written consent, before/after where the profession's rules
forbid it, advice that replaces a visit, prices or ads if their regulator restricts them. Check the local rules for
medical advertising before going live.

## Styles that fit
`/doodle` for explanations, `/vox` for myth-vs-fact with sources, `/vogue` for the person.
