# Personal page / creator in any niche · content playbook

## The viewer
- Follows a person for a promise: useful things, a point of view, entertainment, a journey to watch.
- Follows when the reel delivered and the next one looks likely to deliver again.
- Leaves when the hook lies, the reel drags, or the topic is far from their life (distance ladder).

## Content pillars (weekly mix, adjust to the niche)
| pillar | share | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| useful | 40 % | one thing the viewer can use this week, shown working |
| personal proof | 20 % | what you did/made/tried yourself, with real numbers |
| opinion | 15 % | a clear take on something in the niche, with reasons |
| story | 15 % | a moment from your path, short, with a lesson |
| community | 10 % | answering comments, viewers' questions |

## Reel ideas (shapes; the owner fills the topic)
1. «اگه [هدف] داری، قبل از [قدم] اینو ببین» — call-out + stakes.
2. «[عدد] تا [چیز] که [نتیجه]» — stacked numbers.
3. «خودم امتحان کردم: [نتیجه] در [مدت]» — personal proof.
4. «همه می‌گن [باور]، ولی…» — contrarian.
5. «سؤال یکی از شماها: [سؤال]» — community.
6. «سه تا اشتباهی که من کردم» — story + lesson.
7. «فقط با [منبع کم]، این کار رو بکن» — low effort, real result.
8. «[چیز رایگان] که کسی بهت نمی‌گه» — access.
9. «یه روز از [کار/زندگی]م» — journey.
10. «قبل و بعد از [تغییر]» — transformation.

## Weekly shot list
Talking head (two framings), hands doing the thing, the screen/result, the place where you work, walking shots, the
tools you use, reactions to results.

## Gate words
A word per reel tied to what the reel promised: the file, the list, the template, the links.

## Never
A second voice faking reactions, numbers you can't show, someone else's content without credit, promises the gate
does not keep.

## Styles that fit
All five; let `/reel-style` pick per reel and `/analytics` decide the house style.
