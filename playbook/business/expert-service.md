# Expert / service (teacher, coach, consultant, lawyer, accountant, designer, course) · content playbook

## The customer
- Has a problem they cannot solve alone (an exam, a visa, taxes, a brand, a skill) and is looking for someone they trust.
- Chooses on: does this person clearly know it, did it work for people like me, how much, how long.
- Stops on: price unknown, "is it for my level?", too much jargon, fear of being sold to.

## Content pillars (weekly mix)
| pillar | share | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| give the answer | 35 % | one question your clients ask, answered fully in 40 s |
| mistakes | 20 % | «۳ تا اشتباهی که…» in your field |
| proof | 20 % | a result (numbers, with consent), a case, your own path |
| how it works | 15 % | your process, a session, the course inside |
| the offer | 10 % | the service, the price or range, the next start date |

## Reel ideas
1. «اگه داری واسه [هدف] آماده می‌شی، این رو حتماً بدون» — the key fact.
2. «۳ تا اشتباه رایج توی [موضوع]» — counted.
3. «سؤالی که این هفته ده بار ازم پرسیدن» — answered.
4. «یه نمونه: قبل و بعد از [کار]» — case (consent).
5. «چقدر طول می‌کشه که [نتیجه]؟ واقعیتش» — honest timeline.
6. «یه ابزار/کتاب/قالب رایگان که خودم استفاده می‌کنم» — useful freebie.
7. «یه جلسه با من چه شکلیه» — the process.
8. «چرا [روش رایج] جواب نمی‌ده» — contrarian, with reasons.
9. «از کجا شروع کنم؟» — the first step.
10. «ثبت‌نام دوره‌ی بعد» — offer with date.

## Weekly shot list
Talking head at the desk, writing on a board/paper, the screen of the work (their own), a session (consent), the
certificate/book/tool, walking to the office, hands on the keyboard.

## Proof
Credentials with a public link, real results with numbers and consent, years and number of clients from their records,
published work.

## Gate words
| word | they get |
|---|---|
| راهنما | a checklist/PDF answering the reel's question |
| مشاوره | booking link for a call |
| دوره | the course page + next start date |

## Never
Guaranteed results («۱۰۰ درصد قبولی»), fake testimonials, legal/financial advice beyond their licence, clients' private
details.

## Styles that fit
`/swiss` for mistakes and lists, `/vox` for cases with numbers, `/vogue` for their story, `/doodle` for how-tos,
`/hud` if the work happens on screens.
