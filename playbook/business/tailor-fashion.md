# Tailor / fashion designer / clothing boutique (in person) · content playbook

## The customer
- Wants something that fits and feels personal: an event outfit, a bridal dress, a suit, everyday pieces that fit right.
- Chooses on: the designer's style, fit on real bodies, fabric quality, price, time to deliver.
- Stops on: fear it won't look like the photo, price unknown, too far, long waiting time.

## Content pillars (weekly mix)
| pillar | share | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| from fabric to piece | 30 % | the cut, the sewing, the fitting, the final look |
| on real people | 25 % | the finished piece on the client (consent), how it moves |
| styling & fit tips | 20 % | which cut for which body, colours, fabric care |
| the atelier | 10 % | the designer, the workshop, the tools |
| practical | 15 % | prices, delivery times, how a custom order works |

## Reel ideas
1. «این لباس از یه طرح ساده تا روز مراسم» — the process.
2. «کدوم یقه واسه کدوم صورت؟» — styling.
3. «پارچه‌ی خوب رو این‌جوری تشخیص بده» — quality.
4. «سفارش لباس مجلسی چند روز طول می‌کشه؟» — practical.
5. «اشتباهاتی که لباس آماده رو بد می‌کنه» — fit.
6. «پرو اول، پرو دوم، تحویل» — the journey.
7. «یه کت، سه جور ست» — styling.
8. «پشت‌صحنه‌ی آتلیه» — workshop.
9. «قیمت دوخت [لباس] و چرا» — pricing.
10. «لباس این عروس (با اجازه‌ش)» — the story.

## Weekly shot list
Scissors through fabric, the sewing machine needle close up, chalk lines, the measuring tape, the fitting in the mirror,
the dress turning, fabric rolls, the designer sketching, the finished piece on a hanger in light.

## Proof
Real clients' finished pieces (consent), the fitting process, fabric labels, delivery dates kept.

## Gate words
| word | they get |
|---|---|
| قیمت | price ranges + how custom orders work |
| سایز | the measuring guide |
| ست | a styling guide for the reel's piece |

## Stories
Work in progress, a poll between two fabrics, open slots this month, Q&A.

## Never
Clients' photos without consent, copying another designer's work as theirs, "ready in 2 days" if not true.

## Styles that fit
`/vogue` (the natural fit), `/doodle` for fit and measuring guides, `/swiss` for prices.
