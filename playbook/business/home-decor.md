# Home decor / furniture / interior design / cabinets & renovation · content playbook

## The customer
- Moving, renovating, or tired of their space; buys big, rarely, and fears regret.
- Chooses on: seeing it in a real room, quality up close, price, delivery and installation, the designer's taste.
- Stops on: "will it fit / match my room?", price unknown, delivery fears, workmanship doubts.

## Content pillars (weekly mix)
| pillar | share | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| room transformations | 30 % | before → after of a real project (owner consent) |
| tips for small spaces | 25 % | layout, light, colour, storage, renter-friendly ideas |
| product in a real room | 20 % | a piece styled, measured, opened, used |
| how it's made / installed | 15 % | the workshop, the installation day |
| practical | 10 % | prices, delivery, warranty, consultation booking |

## Reel ideas
1. «یه پذیرایی ۲۰ متری، قبل و بعد» — transformation.
2. «۳ تا اشتباه چیدمان که خونه رو کوچیک‌تر نشون می‌ده» — tips.
3. «این مبل رو از نزدیک ببین» — quality.
4. «با [مبلغ] چطوری اتاقت رو عوض کنی» — budget.
5. «رنگ دیوار واسه خونه‌ی کم‌نور» — tip.
6. «یه روز نصب کابینت» — process.
7. «اجاره‌نشینی؟ این تغییرها بدون آسیب به دیواره» — renters.
8. «جنس کابینت: [نوع] یا [نوع]؟» — comparison.
9. «طراحی تا اجرا، یه پروژه» — the story.
10. «مشاوره‌ی طراحی ما چطوریه» — practical.

## Weekly shot list
Wide room shots at the same angle before/after, the detail of a joint or fabric, a drawer closing softly, light changing
through the window, the measuring tape on a wall, the installer at work, the van/delivery.

## Proof
Real projects (consent), measurements, materials named, warranty in writing, delivery dates.

## Gate words
| word | they get |
|---|---|
| چیدمان | a small-space layout guide |
| قیمت | catalogue + prices |
| مشاوره | the booking link |

## Stories
Projects in progress, polls between two finishes, new stock, Q&A about a reader's room photo (with permission).

## Never
3D renders presented as finished real projects (label them), clients' homes or addresses identifiable without consent,
copied designs.

## Styles that fit
`/vogue` for transformations, `/doodle` for layout tips, `/swiss` for prices and mistakes.
