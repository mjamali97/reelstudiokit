# Restaurant / café · content playbook

## The customer
- Lives or works within ~20–30 minutes, or orders delivery; decides in seconds from how the food looks and the price.
- Chooses on: taste they can imagine, portion vs price, the vibe of the place, cleanliness, wait time.
- Stops on: price unknown, "is it worth going there?", parking/location, fear the photo lies.

## Content pillars (weekly mix)
| pillar | share | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| the dish | 35 % | one dish, shot so you can almost taste it: cut, pour, steam, cheese pull |
| behind the counter | 20 % | the kitchen making it, the chef's one trick, opening prep |
| price & value | 15 % | «با X تومن اینجا چی می‌خوری», portion on a scale, the combo |
| people & place | 15 % | the owner, a regular (with consent), the corner everyone photographs |
| questions & occasions | 15 % | «برای تولد میز رزرو می‌کنید؟», vegetarian options, the new seasonal item |

## Reel ideas (hooks in the owner's voice)
1. «اگه اطراف [محله] دنبال یه [غذا] درست‌وحسابی هستی، اینو ببین» — the dish, start to plate.
2. «با [مبلغ] اینجا این‌ها رو می‌خوری» — everything on the table, price on screen.
3. «سسِ [غذا]مون رو این‌جوری درست می‌کنیم» — process, one secret that is safe to share.
4. «پرسفروش‌ترین غذای این ماه» — ranking 3 → 1, counted.
5. «یه روز از ساعت ۷ صبح توی آشپزخونه‌ی ما» — time-lapse of the day.
6. «سه تا چیز که هیچ‌وقت توی [غذا]مون نمی‌ریزیم» — quality through what you don't do.
7. «مشتری همیشگیمون چی سفارش می‌ده؟» — a regular, with consent.
8. «[غذای جدید] از امروز اومد» — the launch, the first plate, the price.
9. «جواب سؤال پرتکرار: [پارکینگ / رزرو / بیرون‌بر]» — the answer, the map, the hours.
10. «اینجا با [تعداد] نفر چقدر خرج می‌کنی؟» — the group bill, honest.

## Weekly shot list
Steam rising, the cut, the pour, cheese/sauce pull, plating top-down, the grill flame, cups lined up, the door opening,
the street sign, hands passing a plate, the menu board, the cashier smiling, the empty room before opening.

## Proof
Real prices on screen, the full plate (no hidden sides), queue at lunchtime, delivery-app rating screenshot (current),
the kitchen's cleanliness.

## Gate words
| word | they get |
|---|---|
| منو | the menu with prices (image or link) |
| رزرو | the booking link or number + hours |
| کد | a discount code for the first order, with its end date |

## Stories (daily)
Today's special (link sticker to order), a poll «کدوم رو هفته‌ی بعد بیاریم؟», a question box for reservations, the queue
right now, "sold out" honesty.

## Captions
Dish name + one sensory line → price → order/booking → address + hours → 5 hashtags: city/neighbourhood + dish + occasion.

## Never
Food photos that are not the real served plate, fake "sold out" urgency, health claims («رژیمی»، «بدون کالری») without a
basis, customers' faces without consent.

## Styles that fit
`/swiss` for prices and lists, `/doodle` over real footage for a recipe, `/vogue` for the chef's story.
