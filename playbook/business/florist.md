# Florist / plant shop / gifts · content playbook

## The customer
- Buys for a person and a moment (a birthday, an apology, a visit, a ceremony) or for their own home.
- Chooses on: how it looks, freshness, price for the size, same-day delivery, a personal touch.
- Stops on: "will it look like the photo?", price unknown, delivery time, does it last.

## Content pillars (weekly mix)
| pillar | share | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| making the bouquet | 35 % | from loose stems to the wrapped bouquet |
| by budget / occasion | 20 % | «با [مبلغ] چه دسته‌گلی می‌شه گرفت», what fits which moment |
| care tips | 20 % | how to make flowers last, plant care |
| reactions & deliveries | 15 % | the delivery moment (consent) |
| practical | 10 % | prices, delivery area and times, how to order |

## Reel ideas
1. «یه دسته‌گل از اول تا آخر توی ۳۰ ثانیه» — process.
2. «گل‌ها رو این‌جوری بیشتر نگه دار» — care.
3. «با [مبلغ] سه جور دسته‌گل» — budget.
4. «کدوم گل واسه کدوم مناسبت؟» — guide.
5. «گلدونی که توی خونه‌ی کم‌نور زنده می‌مونه» — plants.
6. «سفارش امروز، تحویل امروز: چطوری؟» — practical.
7. «صبح زود توی بازار گل با ما» — behind the scenes.
8. «اشتباهی که گل رو زود پژمرده می‌کنه» — mistake.
9. «یه سفارش خاص این هفته» — the story.
10. «واکنش وقتی گل رسید» — consent.

## Weekly shot list
Stems being cut at an angle, wrapping paper folding, the ribbon tie, the finished bouquet turning, water drops on petals,
the shop window, the delivery handover (consent), plants in light.

## Proof
Real bouquets at the shown price, delivery times kept, reviews (consent).

## Gate words
| word | they get |
|---|---|
| قیمت | bouquets by budget + order link |
| نگهداری | the care guide |
| مناسبت | which flowers for which occasion |

## Stories
Today's flowers with prices, a poll between two bouquets, same-day delivery cut-off time, Q&A.

## Never
Photos of bouquets they did not make, prices that hide the delivery fee, recipients' faces without consent.

## Styles that fit
`/vogue` for signature arrangements, `/doodle` for care tips, `/swiss` for prices by budget.
