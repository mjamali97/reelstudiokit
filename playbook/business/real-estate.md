# Real estate agent / property consultant · content playbook

## The customer
- Buying, renting or selling in a specific area; anxious about price, scams and making a big mistake.
- Chooses on: knowledge of the neighbourhood, honesty about prices, speed, the agent's trust and track record.
- Stops on: fear of being overpriced, hidden fees, fake listings, pushy agents.

## Content pillars (weekly mix)
| pillar | share | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| property tours | 30 % | one home, walked through in 40 s: light, layout, the honest downside, the price |
| area guides | 20 % | a neighbourhood: prices per metre, schools, transport, what it is like at night |
| buyer/renter education | 25 % | contracts, deposits, checks before signing, common traps |
| market reality | 15 % | what changed in prices this month, from their own deals or official data (cited) |
| the agent | 10 % | who they are, how they work, fees stated plainly |

## Reel ideas
1. «با [مبلغ] توی [محله] چی می‌شه اجاره کرد؟» — three real options.
2. «قبل از امضای قرارداد اجاره این ۵ تا رو چک کن» — checklist.
3. «این خونه یه ایراد داره که آگهی نمی‌گه» — honesty builds trust.
4. «[محله] واسه کی خوبه، واسه کی نه» — area guide.
5. «قیمت متری [محله] این ماه» — cited numbers.
6. «سه تا کلاهبرداری رایج آگهی‌های ملک» — protection.
7. «تور ۴۰ ثانیه‌ای یه آپارتمان [متراژ]» — the tour.
8. «کمیسیون مشاور املاک دقیقاً چقدره؟» — fees, per the official rule where they work.
9. «فروشنده‌ای؟ این سه کار قیمتت رو بالا می‌بره» — sellers.
10. «یه روز با من سر سه تا بازدید» — behind the scenes.

## Weekly shot list
Door opening, walking through each room (gimbal or steady), the view from the window, the kitchen details, the building
entrance, the street, the agent talking to camera in the area, the map with the location (no exact address for safety).

## Proof
Real listings (with the owner's permission), deals closed (no private data), official price data with its source,
licence/registration where required.

## Gate words
| word | they get |
|---|---|
| لیست | current listings in the budget they comment |
| چک‌لیست | the pre-signing checklist |
| محله | the area guide PDF |

## Stories
New listings, a poll (which area next), "sold/rented" updates, Q&A about contracts.

## Never
Exact addresses or owners' private details, fake or already-sold listings as bait, legal advice beyond their licence,
price claims without a source.

## Styles that fit
`/vogue` for premium tours, `/swiss` for checklists and prices, `/vox` for market stories with numbers.
