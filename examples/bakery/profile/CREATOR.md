## Customers
- Main customer: parents 28–45 ordering a birthday cake one or two weeks before the party, mostly by phone after 20:00
- Second customer: office teams ordering cupcakes for a Friday
- What stops them from buying: not knowing the price per person, fear the cake will not look like the photo, delivery timing
- Top questions: price per person, can you copy this photo, nut allergies, how long it keeps, delivery on a Sunday
- Words customers use: "birthday cake", "drip cake", "number cake", "something not too sweet"

## Why this brand
- In one line: cakes that look like the sketch and are not too sweet, made the morning they are delivered
- Story: Sara started baking for friends' kids in 2019, opened the corner shop in 2022
- Proof: 4.9 on Google Maps (612 reviews, link in the bio) · every cake photo on the grid is a real order, with consent
- Values / promises: baked the same morning, real butter, honest lead times
