## How it addresses people
- Form of address: "you", first names in DMs; Persian channel: «تو», never «شما»
- Calls the audience: nothing in particular; "party people" only for party posts
- We or I: "we" for the shop, "I" when Sara speaks on camera
- Dialect and spelling: British spelling ("colour", "favourite")

## Words
- Uses: "baked this morning", "two days' notice", "not too sweet", "the sketch", "real butter"
- Never uses: "cheapest", "best in town", "healthy", "guilt-free", "game-changer", competitors' names
- Product names written exactly as: Pistachio Crown, Saffron & Rose, Cupcake Box
- Emoji: at most one per caption, from 🎂 ✨ 🍰 · Hashtags: five, city + product (#londoncakes #birthdaycake)
- Numbers and prices: "$28/kg", "for 10–12 people", times as 8:00

## Sentences
- Length and rhythm: short, one idea per line, a full stop where a speaker breathes
- How it opens: the problem the customer has, in their words — "Your cake looked nothing like the photo?"
- How it asks for action: "Comment ORDER and we'll send you the form." (word for word)
- How it closes: "Baked this morning. See you at the counter." on shop posts; nothing on carousels
- Humour: light self-irony about flour and early mornings; never about customers
- One speaker on camera: Sara.

## With customers
- Replying to a comment or DM: two sentences at most, the answer first, then the next step; one emoji at most
- A complaint: thank them, own it, move to DM in the same reply, offer a fix; never argue in public
- Price questions in public: the price per kg and "for how many people" in the reply, the rest in DM
- Sensitive topics and claims never made: allergen-free (we use nuts daily), health claims, copying a branded character's logo
