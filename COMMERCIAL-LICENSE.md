# Licensing: who uses Reel Studio for free, who needs a commercial licence

Reel Studio 2.1.0 and later are licensed under the **Business Source License 1.1** (`LICENSE`). In plain words (the
`LICENSE` text is what counts):

| you are | you may | licence |
|---|---|---|
| a business, a brand, a shop, a clinic, a creator, a non-profit, making content **for yourself** (or as its employee) | use it in production, for free, change it, keep your changes private | the free Additional Use Grant |
| anyone trying it, learning, testing, contributing | everything except production use for others | free (non-production use) |
| an **agency, studio or freelancer** making, editing, publishing or managing content **for clients** | use it for your clients | **commercial licence** |
| a company offering Reel Studio, or a product built from it, **as a hosted or managed service** | run it for others | **commercial licence** |

Each version becomes **Apache License 2.0** four years after its release (the Change Date in `LICENSE`, 2030-10-12 for this
version), with Apache's patent licence and patent protection, for everyone, including agencies.

Versions **2.0.1 and earlier** were published under the MIT License; copies of those versions stay MIT.
Third-party parts (fonts, icons, illustrations, libraries in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`) always keep their own open licences.

## The agency licence
- covers: production use for your clients, all your staff, unlimited client brands (or per seat, as agreed), updates for the term
- includes: the right to keep your changes private and to white-label the outputs (the reels, posts and reports you deliver)
- does not include: reselling or re-licensing the kit itself, or hosting it as a service for others (that is a separate agreement)
- price and term: on request

**Request one:** open an issue with the **Commercial licence** template on github.com/mjamali97/reelstudiokit (company name,
country, number of client brands; nothing confidential), and the licensor will reply. The licensor may also grant free
agency licences for non-profits and education.
