# /reel-idea · idea → script → styles → edit

Read `profile/CREATOR.md`, `VOICE.md`, `HOOKS.md`, `RULES.md`, `IDEAS-REMOVED.md` first. Then follow
`playbook/IDEA-TO-REEL.md` in order and stop for their choices:

1. Read their business playbook (`playbook/business/<file>` named in CREATOR.md) and verify the idea's facts with
   `playbook/BUSINESS-REEL.md` §1 (prices, dates, stock, claims they can stand behind). Then the distance ladder and
   "can this viewer act on it tonight?".
2. Write it up in their language: two or three hook variants, the full script (five beats, their voice) under the best hook,
   the gate word and what the gate gives, and the **viral score /10** with its breakdown and the weakest part.
3. Suggest **two or three edit styles** (`/reel-style`) with their odds for this content.
4. Wait. They film and pick; then `playbook/TAKE.md` → `STYLE-REEL.md` → `MOTION-QA.md` before showing anything.

Never invent a number, a price, a review or a result. Never start editing before they picked. Save the script to
`reels/<slug>/SCRIPT.md` (a short slug in Latin letters, e.g. `reels/autumn-menu/`): the folder the take, the motion edit, the
cover, the gate and `/publish` use next (`STYLE-REEL.md`, `PUBLISH.md`).
